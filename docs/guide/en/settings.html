# App settings

1. Tap **🛠️** in the top corner to open **Settings**.
2. **Theme**: system, light or dark. **Language**: Tiếng Việt, English, Русский or Français.
3. **When pressing a key** (phone): vibrate or not. **The TV’s keyboard**: whether the [typing box opens by itself] when the TV selects a text field.
4. **App on this computer**: **🔄 Check for a new version** and **Quit** (stops the program; the TV is not affected).
5. Further down: [Home Assistant · Shortcuts], and About has [Send the log].

### Tips

- **Send anonymous stats** (on by default): see [Anonymous stats]. In the phone app it is under Privacy.
