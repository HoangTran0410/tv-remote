# TV Remote user guide (for AI agents)

Remote control for Android TV / Google TV (and LG, Samsung, Roku, VIDAA) with no server in between: a phone app, a computer app, a web page served by the TV, and TV apps. Find the topic below, then read only its page. The same guide for people: https://tvremote.pages.dev/guide

## Get started
- [Who uses it for what]: Find the row like you and open each item for the steps. Most need TV Setup on the TV. _(dung cho ai doi tuong gia dinh tre em homestay khach san phong cho thue quan ca phe cua hang ong ba nguoi gia mat remote use cases who families hotels cafe kids grandparents)_
- [Install the phone app (Android)]: The easiest way: nothing to install on the TV, the keys work at once (see). Turn on ADB for the picture, apps and files. _(tv remote apk dien thoai android)_
- [Install the computer app]: Download the build for your computer (links below) and unpack it. _(mac windows linux zip tar webkit unverified developer)_
- [Keys before ADB is on]: You can still control the TV before ADB is on. The phone and computer apps send keys with Google's Android TV Remote protocol, so many… _(atv remote protocol 6466 6467 khong adb ghep remote ma 6 ky tu bam nut khong an pairing)_
- [Install the app on the TV (TV Setup)]: A small app on the TV: it runs the TV's web page, the sleep timer, app limits and more. _(downloader 2397346 2973135 tv setup apk cai dat)_
- [Open the TV's web page (iPhone, any browser)]: iPhone has no app. Instead the TV serves its own control page. Open it in Safari or any browser. _(iphone safari ma ghep 6 so pairing code web cua tv ios)_
- [Infrared remote (no network)]: A phone with an IR blaster (many Xiaomi, Huawei and older Samsung phones) works like a paper remote: no Wi-Fi, no TV to pick. Phone app… _(hong ngoai ir remote infrared khong mang mat mang remote hong do ma fpt play box dien thoai den ir)_
- [Air Mouse]: Hold the phone like a remote: turning your wrist moves a pointer on the TV, like LG's Magic Remote. Good for apps the arrow keys cannot… _(chuot bay air mouse con tro con quay hoi chuyen gyroscope nhap cuon magic remote lg)_
- [Turn on ADB by hand (if needed)]: Optional, and usually not needed: TV Setup opens the switch itself. Without ADB the phone and computer apps still send keys. ADB adds the… _(adb developer options tuy chon nha phat trien remote start dia chi ip fpt play mat khau password ban dung build box nha mang)_
- [Pair with Wireless debugging (Android 11+)]: Some newer Google TVs only have “Wireless debugging” and no plain ADB port. The phone and computer apps and the TV web page (iPhone too)… _(go loi khong day wireless debugging ghep noi ma ghep noi google tv doi moi canpair)_
- [Connect and “Allow USB debugging?”]: The first time a device connects, the TV asks whether to trust it. This is Android's safety step; once per device. _(allow usb debugging luon cho phep always allow nhin len tv ket noi)_

## Control
- [The remote]: The 🎮 Remote tab works like a real remote. Tap to press. _(phim nguon power home back menu volume kenh channel more keys them phim)_
- [Type on the TV]: Tap ⌨️ Keyboard on the Remote tab: the box opens right under the top row. _(ban phim keyboard go chu co dau tieng viet enter)_
- [The TV's clipboard (accents, pasting)]: The clipboard holds the text the TV last copied. Use it to read it, copy it to your device, or send text to the TV. _(clipboard dan chu copy sao chep dan vao o gõ chu co dau paste)_
- [Voice commands]: The Ask the TV: open YouTube box (in ⌨️ Keyboard) understands plain sentences: type and tap 🔍, or tap Speak. _(giong noi micro hoi tv mo youtube voice assistant)_
- [Quick talk (phone app)]: The voice button on the phone app's remote: hold, speak, let go and the TV does it. _(noi nhanh quick talk giu de noi hold to talk giong noi nut noi dien thoai)_
- [See and touch the TV's picture]: You see the TV's picture on your device and tap or click as if touching the TV. _(pip thu nho noi touchpad cham man hinh xem hinh scrcpy chuot keo cuon watch fullscreen toan man hinh tv khong cho chieu)_
- [Listen to the TV on your device]: Hear the TV in your own headphones without bothering anyone. Needs Android 11 or newer on the TV. _(nghe 🎧 tai nghe am thanh sound audio headphones opus)_
- [Picture quality and “Frame pace”]: Choppy or blurry? Open 🖥️ Picture quality from the quality button next to ▶ Watch. The defaults suit most TVs. _(chat luong hinh nhip hinh bitrate khung giay bo ma hoa sharpest quality frame pace giat lag)_
- [Use a game controller as the remote]: Plug or pair a controller (Xbox, PlayStation…) into the device running the app and drive the TV. _(tay cam gamepad joystick xbox ps5 ps4 controller game pad do nhan)_

## Automation
- [Sleep timer]: Tap More keys → Sleep timer, or open ⏰ Automation → 😴 Sleep timer. _(hen gio tat sleep timer tat man hinh van nghe)_
- [Bedtime]: Tab ⏰ Automation → 🌙 Bedtime. _(gio di ngu bedtime tre em tat tv ban dem)_
- [Welcome screen]: Tab ⏰ Automation → 🏠 Welcome screen, tap Choose a video or picture (mp4, webm, mkv, jpg, png, webp; 200 MB at most). _(man hinh chao welcome video anh khach homestay khach san bat tv khoi dong)_
- [App, volume and memory limits]: Tab ⏰ Automation → ⏳ Daily app limits. _(gioi han app moi ngay am luong ram day limit tre em)_
- [Lock apps with a PIN]: Once unlocked it stays open until another app comes up or the TV turns off; opening it again asks again. 5 wrong PINs: a minute's wait. _(tre em kids google authenticator totp ma du phong recovery tong gio xem khoa app ma pin tre em lock pin parental)_
- [App schedule: days and hours]: For example: YouTube opens only Monday to Friday, 16:00 to 18:00, at most 30 minutes. Outside those hours the app is closed at once and… _(lich mo app theo ngay gio thu hai den thu sau khung gio tre em chan app schedule weekday window parental block)_
- [Scenes (one tap, many steps)]: A scene is a chain of steps run with one tap. For example “Movie”: turn on the TV, open Netflix, set volume 20. _(canh scene lenh nhanh tu dong hoa kich ban)_
- [Control the TV from Telegram]: Message your own bot to turn the TV off or on, set the volume, open apps, take a screenshot. The bot runs in TV Setup and opens no port… _(telegram bot botfather token pair ghep doi nhan tin chat nhom group lenh command shot remote)_
- [Telegram: alerts and bedtime]: More bot commands, and the TV messages you when something happens. A paired chat is needed (see the previous topic). _(telegram bao tin alert thong bao gio ngu bedtime gioi han limit chan app qr ghep doi lich su history usage thoi gian man hinh scenes channel type)_
- [Home Assistant · Shortcuts (Siri)]: Let iPhone Shortcuts, Siri or Home Assistant control the TV with one link. _(home assistant phim tat shortcuts siri hook rest_command yaml webhook ios tu dong hoa nha thong minh token)_

## When something is wrong
- [App settings]: Tap 🛠️ in the top corner to open Settings. _(cai dat settings giao dien sang toi theme ngon ngu language rung vibrate kiem tra ban moi cap nhat tat thoat quit)_
- [The self-check when it will not connect]: Cannot connect? The app checks by itself in under 4 seconds and gives one conclusion with the fix. No guessing. _(tu kiem tra loi diagnose khong ket noi vpn dong ho clock mang khac thu lai quet mang bat adb)_
- [“The browser needs an update”]: The phone app's Manage screen uses Android's own browser (WebView). If it is too old, the app shows this notice. _(webview play store cap nhat trinh duyet android system webview cu old)_
- [Send the log to get help]: Open Settings (🛠️) → About. _(nhat ky log gui loi bao loi wirelog ho tro support ma 8 so)_
