# Voice commands

The **Ask the TV: open YouTube** box (in **⌨️ Keyboard**) understands plain sentences: type and tap **🔍**, or tap **Speak**.

| Say | The TV does |
| --- | --- |
| open Netflix | Opens that app. |
| open the app netflic | With the word “app”: opens the app whose name is closest (a few letters off or split words still work). |
| open cats on YouTube | Searches inside that app (FPT Play, YouTube); an app that cannot be searched is just opened. |
| mute · pause · play | Presses that key. |
| go home · back · next · previous | Navigates. |
| volume up 5 · volume down | Changes volume. |
| turn off · turn on | Power. |
| sleep in 30 · cancel the timer | Sleep timer. |
| open channel BBC News · next channel | Opens it in [TV Channels]. |
| search cats · YouTube | Opens and searches in the app picked at Setup → Remote buttons → Voice commands (SmartTube, YouTube; TizenTube only opens). Also picks which app “YouTube” is. Default unchanged. |
| a scene's name | Runs that scene. |

> 💡 Anything else goes to the Assistant or a YouTube search.

> 💻 Computer app on Windows, Linux: 🎤 sends your voice straight to the TV's Assistant (Android TVs); the TV does the listening.

The TV remote's mic button: turn on **Mic button opens TV Remote voice** in the Setup tab, Remote buttons card (off by default). Needs a newer TV Setup.

A TV with no ADB: the first mic press asks which app to use; pick **TV Setup**, then **Always**.

Mic button still opens Google? Until a press reaches TV Setup, a warning with a button shows under the switch (Undo restores it).

A TV with no speech recognizer (a box without Google): also turn on **Understand speech on the TV itself, no Google needed** there; off removes it.

Vietnamese 39 MB, English 47 MB (**Language** row). **Commands + free speech**: searches come out right, a few seconds slower.

To have TV Setup Voice always listen first (faster, your voice stays on the TV): pick **TV Setup** under “Listen with”, or press the Mic button tile in TV Home's Quick settings.

Wrong language? Pick **Language the mic listens in**.

### Tips

- The phone app has [Quick talk] (hold to talk, with a preview). The phrases are a signed list on the site, with more commands such as close app, home.
