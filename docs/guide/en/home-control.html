# Quick settings (TV Home)

The Settings button (gear) on the top bar opens a panel in Android's look. Big tiles: Customize, Sleep timer.

Small tiles: Network (Wi-Fi name), Inputs (or Bluetooth when the TV has no inputs), Mic button (Google / TV Setup Voice; only when the remote has a mic button), Kids mode.

Kids mode: tap to turn on; when on, a tap (asks the PIN) picks the apps or turns it off. Needs TV Setup (greyed without it).

Round buttons: Clear RAM, TV settings, Turn off the TV (Screensaver, Screen off, Restart, Power off).

Power off on a TV with no ADB: when TV Setup Keys is on (Settings → Accessibility), the TV opens the system's own power menu for you to pick from; it never powers off by itself. When it is off, the TV says how to turn it on.

**Brightness** (sun icon): OK to change it, Left / Right in steps of 10 %, Back when done; 100 % = no tint.

The **Tint color** button beside it: Dark, Blue light filter, Yellow, Sepia, Night red, Blue. Needs a newer TV Setup.

Customize → Look → **Show only plugged-in HDMI/AV inputs** (off by default): empty ports go in More inputs.

TV settings opens even on TVs that hide the standard Settings screen (HY300 projectors, Xiaomi, TCL, Fire TV).
