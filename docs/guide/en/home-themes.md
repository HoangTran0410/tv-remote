# TV Home themes

A theme changes the whole home screen in one press: background, widgets and where they sit (top, left or right column), app tile style (banner, icon, frosted glass), accent colour, columns and rows, live widgets.

1. On the TV: ⚙ → **Quick settings** → **Customize** → **Themes**. Phone, computer: **TV** tab → **Customize TV Home** → **Themes**.
2. Each tile is the home screen in miniature: the real background, where the widgets go, the app grid. Pick one and the TV changes at once.
3. Like the current look: **Save the current look as a theme** and name it. Your photo, video or web page is captured small for its preview.
4. Share: tap ⓘ on a theme → **Export theme** gives a `.tvtheme.json` file; **Import theme** reads it back (**Include photos/videos** carries your own photos and videos, 60 MB each).
5. Send to everyone: ⓘ on your theme → **Share to the store** (phone, computer), enter a display name, **Send**. It shows in **Community** once reviewed; 3 themes a day at most.

| Ready-made | For |
| --- | --- |
| Easy to read, Kids, Guest TV | Big text, a few tiles in the middle, little to learn |
| Art frame, See-through, Vietnam, Fireplace | A picture, a slideshow or a video fills the screen |
| Morning briefing, Daylight office, Tech | Cards of news, weather, calendar, memory, recent apps |
| Gamer, Match day, Cinema, Tết, Bedtime, OLED night | For the occasion, with a fitting live widget (Free RAM, Keep watching, Sleep timer) |

### Tips

- Switching from an unsaved look keeps it as **Before this theme**, so you can go back.
- A video whose last scene differs from its first: turn on **Fade when the video starts over** (Background) so the loop does not jump. Scenery video themes have it on.
- Hold OK on a theme (web: the ⓘ button) to see what it sets.
- Ready-made themes update from the website, no new TV Home needed.
